**Round 8** — Ghalta: recasts Ghalta (6). Patrick: **Vibranium Mining Mech** (T'Challa +2, Zuri +1 on everything), **Storm, Queen of Wakanda**.

**Round 10** — Ghalta: Sol Ring, **Tangleweave Armor (12/12 Germ)**. Patrick: Black Panther drawn; **Idol of Oblivion → 12/12 Eldrazi** (Monument). T'Challa (given flying and +5 by Storm) and Storm attack — Lookout chumps T'Challa, Altisaur blocks Storm.

**Round 12** — Ghalta: **Bite Down — 26 damage kills Storm** (Patrick loses his evasion one turn before the air race would have started landing 23+ a turn). Rishkar. Patrick: casts **Thran Dynamo off the top with Mystic Forge** (more counters; T'Challa 17/17).

**Round 13** — Ghalta: **Rishkar's Expertise draws 26 cards**; free Collective Resistance destroys Hammer of Nazahn and **Ghostly Prison**. Full swing: T'Challa blocks the 26/26 Ghalta and dies; Eldrazi trades with the Germ; Mech, Inspector, Zuri and Black Panther trade into Tyrant, Raptor, Surrak and Imperiosaur. **Patrick 28.** Patrick: recasts T'Challa; **Beast Within on Ghalta**.

**Rounds 14–16** — Ghalta rebuilds from a 30-card hand (Ghalta back for 8, a wall of dinosaurs and elves) faster than Patrick can draw answers (Sword of the Animist, Throne of the High City, Reconnaissance). Two more full swings: **Patrick eliminated in round 16. Ghalta wins.**

## What decided it

- **Ghalta's card advantage.** Shamanic Revelation (7 cards, +20 life), Thickest in the Thicket (2 a turn) and Rishkar's Expertise (26 cards) buried a deck that drew one card a turn.
- **No board wipe drawn.** Vanquish the Horde and an overloaded Winds of Abandon were the answers to a board like Ghalta's; neither showed up in 30+ cards.
- **Losing Storm in round 12** ended the air race one turn before it would have started dealing 23+ a turn.
- **Patrick missed land drops in rounds 4–6** and still killed Captain America off artifact mana. The Inspector + Monument + Vibranium engine worked.
- **The round 6 choice** (defend instead of killing Dina) is debatable: it kept Patrick's board alive, but handed Ghalta a free elimination and Dina's share of the damage.
