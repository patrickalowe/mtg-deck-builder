Earlier fixture success alone could not establish this. Both games won, but the second already had a strong board; these observations do not prove the swaps caused assembly or victory.
- **No measured lead yet:** the frozen feed contains 26 baseline records (9 wins) and 22 candidate records (5 wins, one suspect). Among 22 overlapping pod/game keys, 21 are clean: **five wins each**. Unequal interim completion makes raw percentages misleading. Even matched seeds diverge when deck/pilot decisions change.
- **Fuel and payoff still matter:** casting Jace early sometimes leaves a several-turn wait for −3. Dreadhorde is a recurring source, not a guarantee of immediate setup. Ordinary payoff chains also win without assembling a loop.
- **Blockers do not solve every loss:** token-entry damage can kill through a defensive token board. Prioritize recognizing and answering the damage engine; no hypothetical extra wins have been assigned.

The candidate changes only Synthetic Destiny → Dreadhorde Invasion and Overwrite the Multiverse → Darksteel Angel. These are owned spares, not confirmed physical swaps. Both C9 arms use real-rules Jace, combo-aware play, safe-walker policy, commander-first, smart/politics/keep-tokens. Do not mix these interim figures with older pilot cohorts.
