# Latest 50 physical-deck Forge games: strategy review

Scope: the 50 most recently completed logs by file modification time for the exact physical baseline v0.1-real. INVALID/ABORTED runs and incomplete logs excluded. This is a frozen snapshot; later completions are not retroactively included. These are AI-controlled games, across multiple policies and some repeated seeds, not 50 independent real-life matches.

## Answer

Different decisions could plausibly change some outcomes, and the available policy reruns already show outcome changes. That does not establish a higher overall win rate, and logs alone cannot determine how many of the losses a human would have converted to wins.

## What the fifty records show

- 7 records have no commander cast; all seven end in a logged loss. Absence of a cast is a review trigger, not proof that casting Jace earlier was legal or best.
- 21 records have no resolved Jace -3. This includes wins as well as losses; the ability is not the deck’s only winning route.
- 21 records contain at least one telemetry decline labeled no_tokens_or_targets. This is not proof that all 21 needed an extra token-maker slot; mana, board losses, previous token use and other legal targets need context.
- The logs include ten actual Ajani four-damage resolutions. This screening did not confirm friendly physical-card deaths immediately following them. Do not classify these fifty losses as Ajani self-wipes merely because older examples existed.
- The newest nine completed v2 calibration games cannot be pooled as if they used the same policy as the older C7 logs.

This is enough to inspect development choices, but logged land-play counts are not a complete record of available mana or board control on each earlier turn.

## Better strategy to test

1. Develop Jace when you can use/protect him; establish fodder or an immediate useful action rather than automatically casting him ahead of every other spell.
2. Preserve a reliable fodder route via Saheeli, Ajani, Way of the Healer, a Jace token, or existing token spells. If the required cards/mana were never available, classify that as a consistency problem rather than a sequencing mistake.
3. Arrange the top card before committing the relevant reveal. Reserve Aminatou’s activation for either setup or blink unless another effect legally resets her.
4. Assess the threatening board and known response options before removal, attacks, life payments or wipes. Avoid assuming later-revealed cards were known to the player.
5. Treat the three-card Cadet loop as an available finish when assembled, not a reason to ignore a simpler productive line while waiting for all pieces.

To estimate additional wins credibly, rerun the same physical list and matched pod/seeds under a frozen, recorded policy; inspect changed games and retain the loss flips as well as win flips. Where full state/decision snapshots exist, branch from a decision using only information available at that moment. If branch replay is unavailable, label the counterfactual untested. Do not award hypothetical wins from hindsight.
