## Findings for Claude

- Darksteel Angel has at least one verified save: v0.6 pod16-g4 shows life 1 → -1, followed by the explicit can’t-lose replacement effect. Jace later wins. This supports inspecting the card’s actual board presence rather than only casts; it does not prove the overall package is better.
- Fateful Absence → Junji death trigger → Meren reanimates Patrick’s Memnarch → Memnarch attacks Jace in v0.6 pod18-g3. Exile can avoid enabling this death trigger. This is a concrete reason to examine the queued exile experiment; it does not prove Despark is always castable or superior.
- Both lists still lose to wide token boards and large flying boards. Ghalta is not the sole problem; one-for-one removal cannot cover every observed collapse.
- Three raw-log losses need explicit alternate-reason handling: baseline pod07-g5 and pod08-g5 lose to Angel of Destiny; baseline pod17-g2 loses to 21 commander damage. The eventual winning deck must not be mistaken for the direct eliminator.
- In v0.6 pod17-g1, the final damage table is misleading: The One Ring upkeep life loss finishes Jace. The Ring is not in the deck file. A preceding Jhoira historic-permanent trigger is consistent with obtaining it from an opponent; no card-list change is implied.
- No confirmed new AI misplay is established in this batch. Low -3 counts, unused wipes, life payments and borrowed risky permanents are audit targets. Assess mana, legality, available choices and board state before changing AI or cutting cards.

## Reviewer limitations found (no running process changed)

- The live parser currently captures only “has lost because …”, not “has lost due to …”. Therefore `jaceLoss: null` can hide a known alternate loss condition. This batch reads the original outcome lines instead.
- Damage totals omit non-damage life loss and alternate wins. Life reaching zero is not itself sufficient evidence of elimination when Darksteel Angel is protecting Jace; pod16-g4 proves this.
- Ordinary logs do not name all Jace -3 hits. Candidate follow-up triggers must never be counted as confirmed hit rates.
