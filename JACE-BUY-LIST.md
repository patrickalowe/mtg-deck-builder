## Price method and evidence

Started with the exact deck-file difference: 17 additions minus the three confirmed owned spares = 14 purchases. Used scryfall-sdk with `setAgent('mtg-commander', '1.0.0')` and one batch `Cards.collection(...)` lookup. Node DNS failed in this environment, so the same collection was retrieved via curl with the same agent, followed by sequential Oracle-ID printing searches (no bursts).

Compared all returned English paper printings and available USD nonfoil, foil and etched prices. Required Commander legality and excluded gold/silver borders and digital cards. This excludes the cheaper World Championship Wrath of God. Non-English prices, unpriced copies and individual shop inventory were not compared. Each selected printing is linked in the table. Prices are Scryfall reference values, not a live seller quote; shipping can exceed the savings from buying tiny singles at different stores.

Raw collection and printing snapshots: `analysis/jace-prices/`. Cheapest selections: `analysis/jace-prices/cheapest.json`; reproducible selector: `printings.py`; document generator: `build-list.py`. A missing price is never treated as zero.

Evidence supporting caution: [finished-run analysis](gauntlet/versions/jace/ANALYSIS.md). Toxic Deluge’s zero casts and the timeout-credit issue prevent treating the current simulation headline as a clean purchase justification.
