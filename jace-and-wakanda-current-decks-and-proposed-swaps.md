### Current-deck combo guide — 2026-10-29 19:11

`JACE-COMBO-GUIDE.md` documents 16 practical combinations using only v0.1-real. A real repeatable line already exists: Aminatou −1 blinks Nicol Bolas, Dragon-God; returned Bolas uses Aminatou’s borrowed −1 to blink Aminatou; repeat while Ajani Unrelenting makes Cadets on every loyalty activation. A final Ajani +1 (original or borrowed by a fresh Bolas) grants haste. Requires initial usable Aminatou/Bolas activations and enough loyalty to survive the −1 cost. No Oath of Teferi or Spark Double required for this token line. Ordinary Aminatou/Jace top-deck/blink sequences are finite without a separate reset engine.

Fodder lessons: Ajani can create replacement Cadets, Saheeli can create a Servo from casting Jace, and Jace can exile the planeswalker tokens from empower effects.
